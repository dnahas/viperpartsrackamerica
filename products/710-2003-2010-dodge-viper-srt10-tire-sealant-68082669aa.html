Description

Don't get caught with a flat on your Dodge Viper SRT10!  Originally equipped with runflat tires from 2003-2006, many folks have since replaced them with PS2's, Nittos, or other non-runflat technology.  Great for your ride but not if you get caught with a flat tire in the middle of nowhere.

This is a brand new MOPAR sealant kit as they ship with brand new Vipers.  It is Mopar part #68082669AA.

You may also want to purchase the Mopar compressor (sold separately) as this sealant fit securely into the compressor for easy storage.  The sealant will help with most punctures and some inflation, while the compressor will bring your tire back up to operating pressure until you can get the tire fixed or replaced.
